Coverage and limits

The conservative spatial policy excludes shared or disputed word assignments, cross-locus merges, disconnected pieces and unresolved layout cases. A spelling difference alone is not a rejection. Long gallows and large original rectangles are retained. These checks are conservative screening, not a claim that every accepted word has been visually verified.

The image overlay uses Voynichese's own cropped display image and its exact display rectangles. Original XML pixel coordinates are retained separately for analyses. No original XML rectangle or ZL spelling was changed. The flag taxonomy and the policies are documented in the toolkit (vmsspatial.audit.flags); vmsspatial audit token <id> describes one word.
